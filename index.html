About CHAYAMANA

CHAYAMANA is the flagship annual photography exhibition and competition organized by the Mahanama College Photographic Art Society (MCPAS). It provides a platform for young photographers across Sri Lanka to showcase their creativity, share unique perspectives, and celebrate the art of photography while promoting artistic excellence, technical skill, and innovation.

Frame 01 · Our story

History
OF CHAYAMANA

Since its inception, CHAYAMANA has evolved from a school photography exhibition into one of Sri Lanka's respected inter-school photography events. Organized annually by the Mahanama College Photographic Art Society (MCPAS), the exhibition has provided countless young photographers with the opportunity to display their work, receive professional recognition, and develop their artistic vision.

Each edition reflects the dedication of the organizing committee, the passion of participating photographers, and the continued growth of photographic art within the school community. While embracing modern techniques and evolving styles, CHAYAMANA remains committed to its founding purpose — promoting creativity, encouraging excellence, and inspiring a lifelong appreciation for photography.

The CHAYAMANA photographic exhibition was established under the leadership of Sethika Rathnayake

Frame 03 · The judge

A Visionary Perspective

Mr. Sanka Sammana — President of the National Photographic Art Society of Sri Lanka (NPAS)

An accomplished figure in the field of photography, Mr. Sanka Sammana brings a wealth of experience, expertise and passion to CHAYAMANA 2026. As President of the National Photographic Art Society of Sri Lanka, he has made significant contributions to the photographic community, inspiring photographers and supporting the growth of photographic art in Sri Lanka.

For CHAYAMANA 2026, he joins us as our Chief Judge and Event Coordinator, bringing his distinguished expertise and vision to ensure a meaningful and inspiring celebration of photography.
